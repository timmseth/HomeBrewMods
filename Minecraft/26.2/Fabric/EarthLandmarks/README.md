# Earth Landmarks 1.0.0

Obelisks at the real world's famous landmarks, a travel map that unlocks as you explore, and walled cities.

## Install

Needs [Earth Data World Gen](../EarthDataWorldGen) (and Minecraft 26.2, Fabric Loader 0.19.5+, Fabric API, Java 25).
Put [`earth_landmarks-mc26.2-fabric0.19.5-1.0.0.jar`](earth_landmarks-mc26.2-fabric0.19.5-1.0.0.jar) in `mods`
(server and clients).

## Landmarks

An obelisk at each of 100+ real landmarks: Everest, Fuji, Kilimanjaro, the Grand Canyon, Uluru, Victoria Falls, the
Pyramids, Machu Picchu and many more, built on the ground where the landmark really is.

## Obelisks and travel

- **Discover** a landmark by walking up to its obelisk: a title and a chime, and it's yours.
- **Travel map** (**K**): travel anywhere within 100 km of a place you've discovered (your spawn, obelisks, places
  you've travelled to, and cities of 100,000+ you walk into). Free, no cooldown, and you arrive on finished ground.
- **Landmarks list** in the travel map, sorted by distance, with a guide to each.
- `/earth landmarks`: how many this world has and the nearest five.
- On the Earth spawn screen: **Landmark obelisks** on/off for the new world.

## Walled cities

Every city of 500,000+ people gets a ring wall, towers, gatehouses and a keep with an obelisk, in one of nine
regional styles. `/earth city <name>` tells you where its keep and gates are. On/off per world (**City walls** on the
spawn screen).
